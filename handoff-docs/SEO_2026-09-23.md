### /near/[city] contract

* Radius 6 h. Bands: under 2 h, 2 to 4 h, 4 to 6 h. Sort: drive time
  ascending, ties by name.
* Drive time: `drive_time_cache` road route when the city is `cached`
  in `lib/origins.ts` (NYC, Boston, Philadelphia, Hartford) and the row
  exists; otherwise `lib/distance` estimate (straight line × 1.2 at
  60 mph) marked `≈` and labelled "estimated". The intro states the
  origin coordinates and the method, and the meta description says
  "road drive times" or "estimated drive times" so the SERP snippet is
  honest too. Philadelphia has no cache rows on 2026-09-23 and degrades to
  the estimate automatically (`driveSource: "estimate"`).
* Status: `lib/seasonDates.deriveResortStatus`, the same call the resort
  page and the map panel make, with " · projected" appended when the
  opening date carries the backfill's "(projected)" qualifier.
* Snow: `weather_cache.forecast_json->measured.sfav2_24h_in` (NOHRSC 24 h
  analysis) labelled "Measured to <time UTC>", only while the resort is
  not dormant. v1 rows have no `measured` and show nothing. No v2 rows
  existed on 2026-09-23; the column is feature-detected per row.
* Lift ticket: `ticket_price_adult_min/max` + "Reported <date>" from
  `ticket_price_updated_at`. Zero rows carry a price today, so the line
  is dormant until the price backfill runs; nothing else changes.
* Permanently closed resorts (`operating_status = 'closed'`) and resorts
  without coordinates are excluded. A city with zero rows inside 6 h
  returns 404 (none does today).
* Static params: the 8 `LAUNCH_CITIES`; the other 21 render on demand
  (`dynamicParams = true`) and are cached the same way. ISR 600 s.
* CTA: `/go?city=<code>` for launch cities ("See this Saturday's picks"),
  `/?from=<code>` for the rest (the map knows every origin; `/go` only
  answers for launch cities).
* JSON-LD: `ItemList` (ascending, one `ListItem` per resort) +
  `BreadcrumbList` (Wynla › Ski resorts near <City>).
* OG image: Node runtime so it reads the same cache entry and the count on
  the card matches the page.

## Structured data rules

* Only emit what the page shows. A question, an amenity or an hours spec
  is omitted when its field is null; a null flag is "not verified", not
  "no".
* Label numbers the way the UI does: `≈` estimates, "projected" dates,
  "Reported <date>" prices, "Measured to <time>" snow.
* `openingHoursSpecification` is parsed from `weekday_hours` /
  `weekend_hours` only when the text is a plain `9am-4pm` / `9:30am-4pm`
  range; free text ("varies") emits nothing.
* `aggregateRating` is intentionally not emitted: the platform has no
  reviews yet and a rating built from a handful would over-claim. Add it
  when `resort_reviews` has ≥ 5 reviews for a resort, computed from the
  rows, never a placeholder.
* FAQPage rich results are limited by Google to authoritative sites since
  2023; the block is still read by AI answer engines and Bing, which is
  why it exists. Do not expect FAQ rich snippets in Search Console.

## Monthly check (15 minutes, first Monday)

1. Search Console → Pages: indexed count should be ≈ 395 resorts + 36
   states + 29 near + 5 guides + 10 lists + templates + statics. Anything
   "Crawled, not indexed" for `/near/*` or `/state/*` means thin content:
   check the page has > 5 rows and the intro renders.
2. Search Console → Enhancements: Breadcrumbs and Sitelinks searchbox
   valid; zero "Unparsable structured data". Paste one resort URL and one
   `/near/` URL into the Rich Results Test.
3. `curl -s https://wynla.app/sitemap.xml | grep -c "<loc>"` — the
   count must not drop; a drop means a Supabase read failed at
   regeneration (the sitemap falls back to the homepage only).
4. `curl -s https://wynla.app/near/nyc | grep -o "resorts · Sorted"` —
   page renders and the count precedes it. Repeat for `/near/denver`
   (estimate path) and `/near/philadelphia` (cached city without rows,
   must render with `≈`).
5. Open one `/near/` page on a phone: rows are 44 px tall, no horizontal
   scroll at 375 px, the bottom CTA clears the tab bar.
6. When the price backfill lands: confirm "Lift ticket from $X · Reported
   <date>" appears on `/near/` rows and "How much is a lift ticket" in the
   resort FAQ, and that the date is the backfill date, not "today".
7. When forecast_json v2 rows exist in season: confirm "in 24 h ·
   Measured to <time UTC>" appears only on running resorts.
8. Bump `STATIC_CONTENT_UPDATED` in `app/sitemap.ts` if static copy
   changed this month.
