## The hero policy (`lib/heroSource.ts`)

`heroSourceFor(resort)` returns `{ kind, src, thumb, alt, credit }`:

1. **photo** — only when `hero_image_url` is a public object in **our** bucket
   `resort-heroes` on our Supabase host (allow-list: the `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`
   host plus the production host literal) **and** `hero_image_verified_winter !== false`
   **and** the object is not the one `lib/data/heroDenylist.json` lists for that slug. The
   denylist takes effect on deploy, without the legacy SQL; a photo published later gets
   a new content-addressed object name, so an old entry never blocks it.
   A hotlinked third-party URL (the 2026-05 Ikon / Indy marketing images, or an
   `upload.wikimedia.org` link) is never shown, whatever the column says; that is the
   copyright guard. On 2026-09-23 the live table had 104 storage-hosted heroes and 0
   third-party URLs among active rows, so the guard is currently a safety net, not a
   visible change.
2. **card** — the slug has an entry in `lib/data/terrainCards.json` (all 395 active
   resorts today). Public-domain elevation render with **no drawn-in text** (the page lays
   its own h1 over it, and `object-cover` crops the sides on a phone), labelled
   "Terrain render · USGS 3DEP" on the hero chip and spelled out in the resort page footer.
3. **gradient** — neither; the caller keeps its designed navy gradient.

The photo `credit` goes through `tidyAttribution`, which drops the 2026-06 batch's
"Unknown authorUnknown author or not provided" text and keeps the licence.

Every surface uses it: resort page header, `/today` and `/favorites` rows (resolved once
in `app/today/data.ts`, `row.hero`), `/go` pick cards (thumbnail from `{slug, name}` only,
so cards, never photos, until the ranker carries the hero columns), `/credits`.
`components/HeroImage.tsx` renders it: a chip on the image says "Photo: Author / Licence"
or "Terrain render · USGS 3DEP" (white/70 over the navy scrim on the resort page; in the
compact map panel a navy pill top-left that links to `/credits` with a 44 px tap box,
because the panel has no footer). The legacy `src=` prop still works for ResortPanel
until it switches, but now applies the storage-host check and the denylist.

## Terrain cards

`node scripts/photos/2-terrain-cards.mjs [--limit N] [--slug a,b] [--force] [--no-upload] [--preview N] [--concurrency 4]`

- Source: AWS Terrain Tiles, terrarium encoding, zoom 12, a 3x3 mosaic (768 px, about
  22 km at 43 N) centred on the resort. USGS 3DEP / SRTM, public domain, no key.
  Attribution line on `/credits` and on every card.
- Render: Horn hillshade, sun from the north-west at 45 deg, vertical exaggeration 1.6;
  16:9 window (768x432) vertically centred on the resort, upscaled to 1600x900 with
  Lanczos. Palette: `#0F1530` -> `wn-navy #1E2952` in shadow, `wn-sky #5BAFE6` on lit
  slopes, `wn-offwhite #FAFAF7` snow above a per-resort snow line (halfway up the
  resort's own vertical when base and summit are known; base + 250 m with base alone;
  the window's 75th percentile otherwise; never below the window's 66th percentile so
  mountain country stays navy).
- **Low relief** (under 200 m of vertical, or under 200 m of relief in the window when
  base/summit are unknown; 156 of 395 resorts): snow is painted only within about 1.5 km
  of the resort point (feathered), at the resort's own mid-vertical, and the 66th
  percentile floor is skipped. Without this, bluff and river-valley hills (Afton Alps)
  had the surrounding farmland painted white.
- Three variants per resort, WebP, uploaded to bucket `resort-cards` with
  `Cache-Control: max-age=31536000`:
  - `<slug>-hero-<sha8>.webp`: 1600x900, vignette and scrim only, **no text** (about
    70-150 KB). Resort page header and map panel.
  - `<slug>-thumb-<sha8>.webp`: 800x450 of the hero (about 25-60 KB). 56 px thumbnails
    on `/today`, `/favorites`, `/go`.
  - `<slug>-share-<sha8>.webp`: 1600x900 with the gold rule, resort name, state, brand
    mark and "Terrain render from USGS 3DEP elevation data via AWS Terrain Tiles". For
    share / OG use where the card stands alone; exposed as `terrainCardFor(slug).share`,
    not wired into `opengraph-image.tsx` yet.
  `<sha8>` is the first 8 hex characters of the SHA-256 of the bytes, so a re-render always
  gets a new URL and no browser or optimizer cache keeps an old card.
- `lib/data/terrainCards.json` = `{ base, cards: { slug: { hero, thumb, share } } }`:
  `base` is the bucket's public URL prefix, the variants are object names (about 74 KB raw,
  a few KB gzipped). The script refuses to mix hosts: if `base` differs from the current
  project it exits unless `--force`.
- Resume-safe (skips slugs with a `hero` in the JSON), `--force` re-renders everything
  (about 3 minutes for 395 with concurrency 4). `--no-upload` never touches the JSON.
  Previews (`<slug>-hero.webp`, `<slug>-share.webp`) land in
  `.tmp-photo-candidates/cards-preview/`. Log + report: `scripts/photos/reports/terrain-cards-*`.
- Superseded objects (`<slug>-1600.webp`, `<slug>-800.webp`, which had text drawn in) are
  still in the bucket and no longer referenced; delete them from the Storage dashboard
  whenever convenient.
- Re-run when a resort's coordinates or base/summit change, or after a palette change.

## Commons harvest (Gate 1)

`node scripts/photos/1-commons-harvest.mjs [--limit N] [--slug a,b] [--force]`

Per active resort (read-only PostgREST with the anon key):

1. **Wikidata match**: `wbsearchentities` on the name and two variants, then
   `wbgetentities`; accept the item whose P625 is within 3 km and whose label shares
   identity tokens (Dice >= 0.34, generic words like "ski", "resort", "mountain"
   dropped), ski-class items (P31 Q130003 etc.) ranked above a same-name peak or town.
   An item with no P625 is accepted only with a near-exact name and a ski class.
   Pulls P18 (main image), P373 (Commons category), the commonswiki sitelink.
2. **Candidate pool** from Commons: the P18 file; the category (P373, sitelink, or
   `Category:<label>`) with one level of subcategories (map/logo/sign subcats skipped);
   `list=geosearch` 3 km, namespace 6, `gsprimary=all`; `list=search`
   `"<core name>" filetype:bitmap`.
3. **Gate 1** on `imageinfo` + `extmetadata`: jpg/png; width >= 1600 and w/h >= 1.2;
   licence in {CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, any version} and not NC/ND;
   title not matching map / logo / sign / plan / piste map / trail map / satellite /
   painting / postcard; Commons categories not in the map / logo / painting families;
   `Restrictions` empty; EXIF `DateTimeOriginal` month in May-Oct rejects the file,
   Nov-Apr marks it `winter`, no date keeps it as `unknown` for the vision pass.
   Aerials are kept (the word "aerial" is not excluded).
   **Relevance** for the two loose routes: a geosearch hit must mention the resort's
   name or a ski-area word (ski, lift, slope, snow, lodge, summit ...) in its title,
   description or categories; a title-search hit must carry a ski-area word. Files
   reached through the matched Wikidata item (P18, its category) are trusted. Without
   this, "Bear Creek" returned Alabama creeks and a 3 km geosearch returned fungi.
4. **Rank + download**: source priority (P18 > category > geosearch > search), then a
   winter EXIF month, then resolution. Up to 6 candidates per resort as 1280 px thumbs
   to `.tmp-photo-candidates/<slug>/cand-N.jpg|png`, plus `manifest.json` with
   `{title, url, thumbUrl, author, licence, licenceUrl, sourcePage, exifDate,
   exifSeason, width, height, description, source, distanceM, file, bytes}` per
   candidate, the Wikidata match, the source counts, the Gate 1 rejection tally and an
   `overflow` list (gated files beyond the cap, so a second vetting round needs no
   re-harvest).

Politeness: one request per second per host (Wikidata, Commons API, upload thumbs),
`User-Agent: Wynla/1.0 (https://wynla.app; hello@wynla.app) resort-photo-pipeline` (the
public project inbox, never a personal address), a 25 s AbortController timeout that
covers the body read as well as the headers, retry with back-off on 429/5xx. Resume-safe: a slug with
a `manifest.json` is skipped. Progress: `scripts/photos/reports/harvest-progress.json`;
log: `harvest-log.txt`. A full run is about 70-90 minutes.

## The vetting pass (next step, human + vision)

Inputs: `.tmp-photo-candidates/<slug>/manifest.json` + `cand-N.*`, and
`.tmp-photo-candidates/contact-sheet.html` (opens from disk; click a thumb to choose it,
"Copy chosen.json" exports the choices).

Rubric for a vision subagent, per resort (read the manifest first, then every
`cand-*.jpg`):

- `is_winter`: real snow cover on the ground, not a summer, autumn or mountain-bike scene.
- `ski_context`: lifts, groomed runs, a base lodge, or the resort's skiable face;
  a generic peak 3 km away is not the resort.
- `same_resort`: consistent with the manifest's name, state, description, Wikidata
  label and geosearch distance.
- `no_blockers`: no recognisable person as the subject (personality rights), no
  logo or sign filling the frame, no watermark, no trail map, no frame from space,
  not a painting.
- `beauty`: sharp, well exposed, a composition that survives a 16:9 crop with a
  navy scrim and white text over the lower third.

Score 0-100; >= 75 auto-approve, 55-75 for Saitarn to eyeball, < 55 reject. Write the
picks to `.tmp-photo-candidates/chosen.json`:

```json
[{ "slug": "alta", "index": 2, "reason": "lift line and fresh snow, no faces" }]
```

`index` is the `cand-N` number. One entry per resort; resorts with no acceptable
candidate simply stay on their terrain card.

## Publishing (`scripts/photos/3-publish.mjs`)

`node scripts/photos/3-publish.mjs --chosen .tmp-photo-candidates/chosen.json [--confirm]`

- Re-reads each chosen file's licence and author **live** from Commons and refuses anything
  outside the allow-list, or a CC BY / BY-SA file with no author (add `"author"` to the
  chosen.json entry after checking the file page). Crops the 1280 thumb to 16:9 at
  1600x900 (attention crop), JPEG q82.
- **Dry run (default):** writes `.tmp-photo-candidates/publish-preview/<slug>.jpg` and
  `publish-preview/plan.json` only. No upload, no SQL, no change to `heroCredits.json`.
- **`--confirm`:** uploads to bucket `resort-heroes` as `<slug>-<sha8>.jpg` (content-addressed,
  1-year cache; needs `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in `.env.local`, never printed), then
  appends one statement per resort to `scripts/photos/reports/publish-<date>.sql` and writes
  the credit into `lib/data/heroCredits.json` with the exact `url` it describes:

```sql
UPDATE resorts SET hero_image_url = '<storage url>', hero_image_source = 'supabase',
  hero_image_attribution = 'Author / CC BY-SA 4.0', hero_image_credit = '<Commons File: page>',
  hero_image_alt = '<Name>, <ST> ski resort in winter', hero_image_verified_winter = true,
  updated_at = now() WHERE slug = '<slug>';
```

The script never runs SQL. Until the statement is applied the row keeps its old hero (or the
card), and `/credits` keeps crediting the old photo: it uses a `heroCredits.json` entry only
when the entry's `url` equals the row's `hero_image_url`. Commit `heroCredits.json` with the
publish. ISR (10 min on resort pages, 1 h on `/credits`) picks the change up.

**2026-09-23 run (vetting step, not this package):** `3-publish --confirm` uploaded 59 photos
at 18:07 UTC, and the statements were applied through `scripts/photos/4-apply-publish.mjs`
(a PostgREST apply with a row backup under `scripts/photos/reports/backups/`), so 163
active resorts had a storage-hosted hero at the 18:12 UTC re-check. `4-apply-publish.mjs`
and the backups belong to that step and are not committed on this branch.

## Legacy re-check and denylist (`scripts/photos/4-legacy-recheck.mjs`)

`node scripts/photos/4-legacy-recheck.mjs [--sheet]` (read-only against the database and Commons)

The 2026-06 pipeline checked "is this a winter ski scene" but not "is this THIS resort", and
printed Commons author boilerplate verbatim. For every active row with a storage hero, the
script fetches the recorded Commons file's live metadata and scores it: does the title or
description name this resort, does it name another resort we list or a place outside the
US, how far is the camera (over 25 km = mismatch, within 8 km = match). It writes:

- `scripts/photos/legacy/legacy-recheck.json`: verdict and reasons per hero (2026-09-23:
  146 match, 8 unclear, 9 mismatch of 163).
- `lib/data/heroCredits.json`: each entry refreshed with the live, cleaned author and
  licence and pinned to the exact hero URL.
- `scripts/photos/legacy/legacy-heroes.sql` (**a human applies it** in the Supabase SQL
  editor): `hero_image_verified_winter = false` for every denylisted hero, and the cleaned
  attribution plus source page for the rest (104 statements on 2026-09-23). Every
  statement is guarded by the current `hero_image_url`, so it does nothing to a row whose
  photo has been replaced since.
- `--sheet`: labelled contact sheets of the live heroes in
  `.tmp-photo-candidates/legacy-recheck/` for the visual pass.

`lib/data/heroDenylist.json` is the reviewed list (`{slug: {object, reason}}`); the script
only proposes. 12 entries on 2026-09-23: ten photos of a different resort (for example
49 Degrees North showed Sugar Mountain, NC; Keystone showed Breckenridge), `timber-ridge`
(FAL, copied from onthesnow.com) and `mt-eyak` (the Commons attribution-only template, see
Licence rules). The app skips them on deploy; the SQL makes the database agree.

## Licence rules (what we may show and how)

- Allowed for re-hosting: **CC0**, **public domain** (including PD-US and US
  government works), **CC BY** and **CC BY-SA** of any version. Not allowed: any NC or
  ND variant, GFDL-only, FAL, "fair use", press-kit / media-centre images (editorial-only
  terms), Ikon / Indy / resort CDN images, Unsplash (no re-hosting), Google Places
  (no caching), Mapbox / Google static maps (no storage).
- The Commons **attribution-only template** (`{{Attribution}}`, short name "Attribution")
  is permissive but not on the list, so `mt-eyak` is denylisted rather than special-cased.
  Widening the list is a product decision: add it to the harvest and publish regexes,
  give `licenceUrlFor` a deed link, and remove the denylist entry.
- Attribution (CC BY / BY-SA 4.0 wants title, author, source, licence, and a note that
  the work was modified): the hero chip prints "Photo: Author / Licence" (a link to
  `/credits` in the map panel); the resort page footer adds "resized and may be cropped"
  and links `/credits`; `/credits` lists author, licence (linked to the deed), the Commons
  source page (linked) and the modification note for every photo the hero policy shows,
  grouped by state, preferring the cleaned `heroCredits.json` entry when its URL matches. ShareAlike applies to the cropped
  photo, which we label with the original licence; it does not extend to the page.
- Keep `hero_image_credit` = the Commons `File:` page URL for every new photo
  (3-publish writes it). The 104 heroes from the 2026-06 batch have their source links
  recovered into `lib/data/heroCredits.json` from that batch's `chosen.json`, refreshed
  by `4-legacy-recheck.mjs`; the legacy SQL writes them into the rows.
- Take-downs: `/credits` and `/data-sources` name hello@wynla.app; remove within one
  business day by setting `hero_image_url = NULL` (the card takes over automatically).
- Terrain cards: USGS 3DEP / SRTM through AWS Terrain Tiles is public domain; the
  courtesy line "Elevation data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey and NASA JPL" is
  on `/credits` and every card says it is a render, not a photo.
